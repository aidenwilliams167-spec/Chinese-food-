The Diffences Between Real Chinese Food and Westernized Chinese Food

Category Chinese Food Western Chinese Food
Common Ingredients Bok choy, bamboo shoots, tofu, whole cuts with bones, seafood, and diverse cuts Standardized beef, chicken chunks, regular broccoli, bell peppers, canned/frozen add-ins
Cooking Methods Steaming, boiling, quick stir-frying, braising Deep-frying, heavy pan-frying, thick gravies
Flavor Profile Balanced umami, natural flavors, or distinct regional spice Sweeter, saltier, heavily reliant on sugar, soy-caramel glazes, and MSG
Serving Style Communal dishes shared around a lazy Susan or round table; hot tea or water Individual platters or takeout boxes; cold sodas or iced tea
Cuisine Structure Divided into eight major regional styles (e.g., Sichuan, Cantonese, Shandong, Jiangsu) Generalized into a single, uniform menu
